About CampusConnect

Everything Your Campus Life, One Platform

CampusConnect was built by students, for students — to replace scattered WhatsApp groups and notice boards with one place to discover events, join clubs, and stay in the loop.

Built for GEHU Students Always Free Verified Events Only

Our Mission

To give every student a single, reliable place to discover opportunities on campus — so no hackathon, workshop, or club activity ever gets lost in a group chat again.

Our Vision

A campus where organizers spend less time chasing spreadsheets and more time building great events, and where every student feels connected to a community that matches their interests.

What Makes CampusConnect Different

We're not another notice board — here's what we actually stand for.

Built by Students

Made by GEHU students who were tired of missing events buried in WhatsApp groups — not an outside vendor guessing what you need.

Every Event Is Verified

Nothing goes live until an admin reviews it, so what you see is real, accurate, and worth your time.

Free, No Catch

No subscriptions, no premium tier, no ads. CampusConnect stays free for every student and club, always.

Your Data Stays Yours

We only ask for what's needed to register you for events — nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
